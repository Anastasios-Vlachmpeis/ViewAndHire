How it works

  1. Paste a job listing and generate a question bank with an LLM.
  2. Choose timing, question selection, and recording mode.
  3. Record one continuous session with prep and answer timers.
  4. Review scores, replay with face tracking, and save for later.

Recordings stay on your machine. Only listing text and transcripts are sent to the LLM for question prep and answer scoring.
